Who we are

Ελληνίδες Μανούλες is a channel of Greek family stories on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Contact: el.manoules@gmail.com.

The “Manoules uploader” tool

Manoules uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule the channel’s own videos on the channel’s own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

  • It uses the YouTube API Services with Google sign-in (OAuth) and the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly, only to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, covers and publish times, and confirm which channel it is posting to.
  • It may also use the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok publishing APIs, only to post the channel’s own videos to the channel’s own accounts.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about any other person, and it does not read viewers’ data, comments or messages.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept privately by the channel owner and are used only to talk to the platform they belong to.

By using YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections.

Stories sent to us

If you email us a story, we use your email only to reply to you. We publish a story only after you agree in writing, and we always change names, places and any identifying details. Ask us at any time and we will delete your email and story.
